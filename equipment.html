Brewing Equipment

Discover the tools that bring out the best in every cup, from simple manual brewers to espresso machines.

French Press

MANUAL BREWING

A classic brewing method that creates a full-bodied cup with a rich texture.

How to use: Add coarse coffee grounds and hot water, steep for four minutes, then press slowly.

Pour-Over Dripper

MANUAL BREWING

A simple brewer that highlights the delicate flavours and aromas of your coffee.

How to use: Place a filter inside, add medium-fine grounds, and slowly pour hot water over them.

Espresso Machine

ESPRESSO

Brew concentrated coffee with a bold flavour and a smooth, rich crema.

How to use: Add finely ground coffee to the portafilter, tamp evenly, and pull a shot.

Coffee Grinder

COFFEE ACCESSORIES

Freshly ground coffee helps preserve aroma and lets you adjust the grind for different methods.

How to use: Choose a grind size suited to your brewing method and grind just before brewing.
